**So for these six tools the work is WIRING (a wire card, a small runner and glue, a go-live), not a new engine.** They belong to the "wire the tools" block, not to "engines for coming-soon tools".
**F2 (sorting the 17 by what each needs):**
- **ENGINE EXISTS, needs wiring and a screen: 6 tools.** Currency Converter, Link Analyzer, Content Extractor, Collage Maker, Picture to Table (cell step only), Document Converter.
- **NEW PURE ENGINE NEEDED, a builder card can start now (new files only): 2 tools.** Rephraser (limited), PDF Tools (hard; three cards).
- **NEEDS A MEASUREMENT FIRST (browser or phone capability, NOT measured): 3 tools.** Audio Converter (WAV pieces exist in `src/tools/audioEdit`; other formats unmeasured), Video Converter, and the text-recognition part of Picture to Table (line 6).
- **NEEDS THE REACH LAYER (network, outside AI, permission broker; 7Q, 7R): 4 tools.** Web Search, Video Downloader, Translator (data first), AI Assistant (a door to chat, not an engine).
- **WAITS FOR SOMETHING ELSE: 3 tools.** Video Maker (the video editor), AXON Code (own plan AC0 to AC4), Grammar Helper (the brain's stages, not a separate engine).
(6 + 2 + 2 + 4 + 3 = 17; Picture to Table is counted once, in the first group, and its recognition part is the third.)
**CONSEQUENCE:** the "17 engines in batches" block is really: 2 new engine tools (about 4 cards, one builder batch), 6 wiring jobs that join the 35-tool wiring family jobs, 3 probes (one measurement job answers all three), and 6 tools that wait for later layers. This lowers the number of NEW engines to build and raises honesty about the rest.
