### A17. Library, memory notes, My Dictionary, new chat, manner and progress display (owner, 2026-10-25; short form in roadmap 7D; binding)
(1) **Library** holds three things: NOTES (the owner's own standing instructions to AXON, e.g. "in every message do this"; several allowed), MEMORY NOTES (AXON's own memory), HISTORY OF FILES (files AXON returned and files the owner sent into the chat, both viewable in one place). PROJECTS: not planned (the owner noticed and said his words were unstructured; ask when the owner is ready). Nothing else is added unless clearly useful ("better nothing than spam").
(2) **Memory notes are GENERAL memory filed by topic** (the owner corrected the first design, which was a word-pair list: "it is not just for words"). What AXON receives about an app (Felix) goes under the topic Felix; a taught word goes under that word. Kinds in the engine: meaning, fact, preference, correction. The first Axon models had a simple form (told "this means this", it remembered) but no place to see it: this builds the place. The engine is B03B3d (`src/brain/mind/memory/notes/`); the Library screen is a later AI Studio job after the engine is merged and a design check.
(3) **Consent (Director decision on the owner's delegation; owner may counsel).** The owner said "if AXON receives it, it will save it" and earlier confirmed "I prefer..." is only a PROPOSAL and nothing is saved without a yes. Both are kept: AXON may PROPOSE a note from what it receives; the note becomes KEPT only with a recorded consent: `said-remember` (the owner's own words asked AXON to remember), `yes` (the owner approved the proposal) or `standing-permission` (a rule the owner approved earlier; none exists yet). A note from an if-clause or reason clause is never proposed. The owner can remove, restore, correct and move any note; every correction keeps the old version. The three kind lists in `notesTypes.ts` are labels of the data format, not words matched against speech (FL13 does not apply to them).
(4) **My Dictionary:** a word enters ONLY when the owner says "add this to my dictionary" (a made-up word, a secret phrase or a mock-up in a chat must never enter by itself; it stays a memory note). The owner may import big lists (for example 20,000 to 30,000 words with meanings made by another AI tool; the C18 check applies). The owner's own words are kept apart from real English words, marked by a small indicator; tapping it shows whether an outside AI tool, with internet and the owner's permission, looked the word up and found the same meaning: a BADGE only, never a gate. "Verified" and "no contradictions" are NOT required (a word may have several meanings and contradictions are allowed). `dictionaryCandidate` in the notes engine only LOOKS at a kept meaning note; it writes nothing.
(5) **New chat = fresh start.** AXON looks in its memory notes only when the owner refers to something. When unsure it names the possibilities in ONE question whose every answer settles it, e.g. "Are you referring to the app we were building earlier (Liquid)? If so, say so; if not, here is the definition of liquid: ...". It accepts that it is unsure and addresses the possibilities. The wording is judged by the owner after the brain exists; there is NO style picker ("Axon is Axon; I hate what can be simple made complex").
(6) **Manner:** well-mannered, civilized, exceptionally diligent and intelligent, always looking for the best way, never overwhelmed.
(7) **Progress display for long tasks:** a short note first (what AXON is about to do, like professional AI tools), then a quiet animation with a live one-line summary of the CURRENT step taken from the plan ("packaging files", "organizing the texts"), a few dots, fading slightly. The wave: the letters or a bar dim and brighten from the left to the right like a wave. The line CHANGES when the step changes and is never a fixed phrase (the older app always said "recalling context": hated). One known step with nothing to plan: wait, animate, then give a short summary. AXON never works blindly: the line comes from the plan. Colours: the logo's orange-red, few colours. Animation idea (owner is unsure, keep it simple, no long art work): something like the logo, a cycle of life, a plant growing to a tree and falling back. Built later by AI Studio after the plan and loop exist.
