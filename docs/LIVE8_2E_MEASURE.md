# LIVE8-2e: the 'case' leftover defect, measured (Director, twenty-fourth account, 2026-11-01). Measure only; NO binder change shipped.
## Cause (MEASURED by reading `argumentBinder.ts` rule 4 and by running the real brain with the binder's output logged)
A text slot takes quoted material first, then DETAIL spans (the words after a marker word such as `to`), with the characters of the words already used (a choice word, a value) cut out. For `change hello world to title case` the detail is `title case`; `title` is cut out as the mode; `case` is left and becomes the text. Same for `kebab case`. It is general, not only `case`: `convert hello world to uppercase please` binds `{mode: uppercase, text: "please"}` and `... uppercase now` binds `text: "now"`. The words BEFORE the marker (`hello world`) are not a text source at all. Text Counter works unquoted only because its text sits AFTER the marker (`count the words in hello world`).
## The obvious fix was tried and REJECTED by measurement
Prototype in a scratch copy: a detail span that contains a used choice word is not a text source. It breaks `tests/argumentBinder.test.ts` line 69 ('detail text with the used choice word cut out': `convert hello world upper` must give text `hello world`), a behaviour the binder was designed to have. So this is not a safe binder edit. The other 10 binder-related test files did not change result with the prototype. The binder was restored byte for byte.
## What this means
- The part 1c quote guard is the right protection: Case Converter answers only on quoted text. It also stops `please` and `now` from being answered. Keep it.
- A real unquoted Case Converter needs the OBJECT of the clause (the words between the verb and `to`) as a text source from the Meaning stage. That is a larger job, NOT PLANNED here (7L), and not worth it while the owner's rule is that quoted text is the thing to work on.
## Q8 for the owner (yes or no; Director's default: yes)
Case Converter keeps requiring quote marks. When the text is missing, D2 asks: "Which text? Put it in quote marks, like convert "hello" to uppercase." LIVE8-2e is therefore CLOSED as: known, guarded, no binder change.
