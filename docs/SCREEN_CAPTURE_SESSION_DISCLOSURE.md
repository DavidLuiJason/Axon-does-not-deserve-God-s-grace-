# AXON Screen Capture Session — Required User Disclosure

**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** This disclosure must be displayed prominently inside AXON before the user starts a screen-capture session. It must not be hidden only in Settings, a privacy policy or terms.

## Screen capture session

When you start, AXON records the visible device screen across apps for this session. The recording is saved in AXON private app storage and is not sent to an AI or external service by this engine. It may include private messages, account information, or other sensitive content visible on screen. No microphone or call audio is recorded. Android shows a persistent recording notification; you can stop from AXON or that notification. Secure or protected content may be blank or unavailable.

The user must take an affirmative action to accept this disclosure. Android will also show its own MediaProjection system consent prompt for each capture session. On Android 13 and newer, AXON also requests notification permission so the recording notification and stop control can remain visible.

Declining the disclosure, notification permission or system screen-capture prompt must leave capture off. A later session requires a new system consent prompt.
