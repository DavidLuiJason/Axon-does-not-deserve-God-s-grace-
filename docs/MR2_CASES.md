## Director decisions (overrulable; the design left them open)
D1 **The composer writes NO words of its own** (FL13: no stored phrases). Every word in the reply comes from a piece: `own` (the piece's own words, for the echo), `text`, `question`, `holdLine`, `line`. The composer only orders, joins and adds the characters `: ` and a blank line. A later wording job (DLG2) and the wiring (MR3) supply the words. D2 Q1 default: answered parts in the order asked. D3 Q2 default: answered parts first, then the question. D4 Q3 default: ONE question at a time: the first asking piece is `pending` and shows its `question`; every later asking piece is `held` and shows its `holdLine` instead (the dialogue holds one pending question). D5 Q4 default: with two or more pieces every part starts with its own words and `: `; with exactly one piece there is no echo and the text is the piece's text alone. D6 order of the reply: answered, then the pending question, then the held lines, then waiting and failed pieces in the order given. D7 at most 6 pieces. D8 all strings must be non-empty with no space or newline at the start or end (inner newlines are fine). D9 a `failed` piece carries `reason` for the trace; it is never printed.

Every piece appears in exactly one of: `answered`, `pending`, `held`, `waiting`, `failed`: nothing is dropped silently (FL15 rule 3). All frozen deep, the error result too; inputs not changed.
